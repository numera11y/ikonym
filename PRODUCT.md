# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

TypeScript + Astro static output for localized pages, with Vite retained for the library build.

## Users

Developers integrating generated SVG avatars; designers exploring visual systems; product and project leads evaluating an avatar approach; and digital-watch readers following generative interface tools.

## Product Purpose

Generate deterministic avatars from a string seed and make the generator usable as a public library and an interactive web demo.

## Positioning

An open-source, deterministic SVG avatar library for product teams: developers can integrate it, designers can explore motif and palette variations, project leads can assess the result, and digital-watch readers can inspect a generative-avatar example. Distributed under MIT; redistributed copies must retain its copyright and license notices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Initial output is SVG.
- Avatar composition is configurable as a 3×3, 5×5, 7×7, 9×9 or 12×12 tiled pattern, with a square-to-round outer-corner control.
- Every tile touches its neighbors to fill the avatar edge-to-edge; bold seed-selected colors, tile architecture and orientations create each motif.
- Orthogonally adjacent tiles never share the same motif family, regardless of color or orientation.
- Same seed and options produce the same avatar, pattern layout and color palette.
- Avatar colors are decorative; text and controls in the fixed interface retain their accessible contrast.
- Generation runs in the browser or calling environment without transmitting the seed to a server.
- Future DiceBear style compatibility and listing are goals, not yet delivered or guaranteed.
- Ikonym is the chosen product and package name; verify registry, domain and trademark availability before release.

## Brand Commitments

The user confirms the name Ikonym and the tagline “des avatars géométriques générés à partir d’un identifiant.” The accepted visual language is a hash-selected, modular geometric mosaic that mixes shell-inspired fans, concentric arcs, curved growth bands and faceted plates within each avatar. The interface uses raspberry as its strong accent, and the logo carries three consecutive colored geometric motifs. Keep the existing site layout, palette and typography stable; omit detached specks and pinpoints. Do not claim that generated forms reproduce a specific cultural tradition.

Ikonym is a Numera11y product intended for its digital-tools catalogue. Public copy should prioritize practical integration, deterministic output, visual evaluation, and the open-source MIT terms. Explain that redistributed versions must preserve copyright and license notices; do not claim commercial resale is prohibited.

## Evidence on Hand

Reference products: Jazzicon, Jdenticon, identicon.js, Gravatar, kydenticon, and DiceBear Patchwork. The user supplied geometric mosaic examples, prefers bold colors and seamless tile joins, and confirmed the Ikonym name.

## Product Principles

- Deterministic tiled geometry and vivid color permutations from a seed.
- Lightweight, developer-friendly integration.
- Local generation without seed transmission.
- Distinctive, playful tessellations with exact edge-to-edge coverage.
